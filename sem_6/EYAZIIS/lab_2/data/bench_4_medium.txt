Medium benchmark text 4: Music education and practice.

Learning to play a musical instrument requires regular practice. Teachers often advise students to divide a difficult piece into short fragments and to work on each fragment slowly. It is better to play a passage correctly several times than to repeat the same mistake without analysis. A practice diary helps the student to plan the week and to fix small achievements.

Modern technologies also influence music education. Video lessons and interactive applications can demonstrate finger positions and rhythm patterns. Nevertheless, a human teacher remains important. The teacher listens to the sound, watches the posture of the student, and provides feedback that software cannot yet fully reproduce. In this benchmark document the text is longer and the vocabulary is slightly more varied than in the short files.
