Music Genres and Their Characteristics

Music can be divided into many genres and subgenres. Classical music refers to art music rooted in the tradition of Western culture. It includes symphonies, concertos, and sonatas composed by musicians such as Beethoven, Mozart, and Bach. Jazz originated in the United States and combines African rhythms with European harmony. Improvisation is a key element in jazz performance. Rock music developed from rock and roll in the nineteen sixties and typically features electric guitars, drums, and strong beats. Pop music is designed for broad appeal and often dominates the charts. Electronic music is created using synthesizers and computers. Hip hop emerged from street culture and includes rapping and sampling. Folk music reflects the traditions of a culture and is often passed down orally. Each genre has its own history, instruments, and audience.
