Musical Instruments and Ensembles

Orchestral instruments are divided into families. The string section includes violins, violas, cellos, and double basses. Musicians play these instruments with a bow or by plucking the strings. The woodwind section contains flutes, oboes, clarinets, and bassoons. Brass instruments include trumpets, trombones, French horns, and tubas. The percussion section provides rhythm and colour with drums, cymbals, and timpani. A full orchestra may have over ninety musicians. A chamber ensemble is smaller and might feature a string quartet or a piano trio. In jazz bands, the saxophone and trumpet often play the melody while the piano and double bass provide harmony and rhythm. The guitar is central to rock and folk music. Digital instruments and software have expanded the range of sounds available to composers and performers.
